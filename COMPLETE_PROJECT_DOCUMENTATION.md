## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Rationale

Traditional indoor human sensing systems rely on optical cameras, wearable sensors (IMUs, smartwatches), or RFID tags. These methods face major real-world constraints:
1. **Privacy Intrusion:** Optical cameras are strictly prohibited in private environments (bathrooms, bedrooms, healthcare facilities).
2. **User Non-Compliance:** Elderly individuals or patients frequently forget to wear tags or charge smartwatches.
3. **Line-of-Sight (LoS) Vulnerability:** Infrared (PIR) and optical sensors fail when obstacles occlude the target.

This project implements a **device-free, camera-free, radio-frequency indoor sensing system** utilizing **Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI)** extracted from an **ESP32-S3** microcontroller. By measuring subtle multipath variations and Doppler frequency shifts caused by human body reflections on Wi-Fi subcarrier channels, the system accomplishes:
- Binary presence detection (Empty Room vs. Human Occupied)
- Human activity classification (Standing, Sitting, Walking)
- 2D room localization across discrete spatial zones and continuous $(x, y)$ coordinates
- Movement velocity and 4-quadrant direction tracking (North, South, East, West)
- Real-time 2D Digital Twin visualization via a web dashboard

## 2. Mathematical Foundations of Wi-Fi CSI Sensing

### 2.1 OFDM Physical Layer & Channel Frequency Response (CFR)
Modern Wi-Fi protocols (802.11n/ac/ax) employ **Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM)**, which divides a wideband channel (e.g., 20 MHz) into multiple orthogonal narrowband subcarriers.

In wireless propagation, the transmitted signal $X(f, t)$ undergoes multipath reflection, scattering, and diffraction from walls, furniture, and human bodies before reaching the receiver:

$$Y(f, t) = H(f, t) \cdot X(f, t) + N(f, t)$$

Where:
- $Y(f, t)$ is the received signal spectrum.
- $H(f, t)$ is the **Channel Frequency Response (CFR)**.
- $N(f, t)$ is Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN).

The channel transfer function sampled at subcarrier index $k$ is reported as a complex number:

$$H(k) = I(k) + j \cdot Q(k)$$

Where $I(k)$ and $Q(k)$ are the In-phase (Real) and Quadrature (Imaginary) components stored as signed 8-bit integers (`int8_t`) in ESP-IDF.

### 2.2 Amplitude and Phase Extraction
For each subcarrier $k$:

$$\text{Amplitude: } |H(k)| = \sqrt{I(k)^2 + Q(k)^2}$$

$$\text{Raw Phase: } \phi(k) = \arctan2\left(Q(k), I(k)\right)$$

### 2.3 Multipath Dynamic vs. Static Decomposition
The total CSI response at time $t$ across all multipath trajectories can be modeled as:

$$H(k, t) = H_s(k) + \sum_{p \in P_{\text{dynamic}}} \alpha_p(t) \exp\left(-j 2\pi f_k \tau_p(t)\right)$$

- $H_s(k)$: **Static component** generated by stationary walls, floor, and static furniture.
- Dynamic component: Attenuation $\alpha_p(t)$ and path delay $\tau_p(t)$ modulated by the human body moving through the Fresnel zones.

## 3. Feasibility Analysis & Hardware Constraints

### 3.1 What ESP32 CSI Actually Delivers vs. False Assumptions

| Property | True ESP32-S3 Hardware Capability | Common False Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| **Subcarrier Count** | 56 subcarriers (20 MHz HT-LTF) | 64 or 128 full independent channels |
| **Precision** | Signed 8-bit integer (`int8_t`), ~48 dB dynamic range | 32-bit floating point precision |
| **Phase Cleanliness** | Heavily corrupted by CFO, SFO, and PDD | Perfect phase angle directly usable |
| **Localization Precision** | Zone-level (~0.8m – 1.5m median error) | Centimeter-level (<5cm) |
| **Single-Link Spatial Info** | 1D projection of multipath channel | Full 3D room coordinates |

### 3.2 Why Phase Sanitization is Mandatory
Raw phase measurements $\phi_{\text{raw}}(k)$ suffer from:
1. **Carrier Frequency Offset (CFO):** Slight oscillator mismatch between phone and ESP32.
2. **Sampling Frequency Offset (SFO):** Clock drift in ADC sampling.
3. **Packet Detection Delay (PDD):** Timing uncertainty in frame preamble detection.

The sanitized phase $\phi_{\text{sanitized}}(k)$ is obtained via linear regression detrending:

$$\phi_{\text{sanitized}}(k) = \phi_{\text{raw}}(k) - (a \cdot k + b)$$

Where $a$ and $b$ minimize $\sum_{k} (\phi_{\text{raw}}(k) - (a \cdot k + b))^2$.

## 5. ESP-IDF Firmware Implementation Details

### 5.1 FreeRTOS Non-Blocking ISR Design
The CSI callback registered with `esp_wifi_set_csi_rx_cb()` runs within the high-priority Wi-Fi driver task. Performing I/O operations or mathematical calculations inside this callback will drop packets or trigger FreeRTOS watchdog panics.

**Firmware Flow:**
1. `wifi_csi_cb()` executes in ISR context: Copies metadata and the raw `int8_t buf[]` into a `csi_packet_t` struct and executes `xQueueSendFromISR()`.
2. `csi_process_task()` pinned to **Core 1** pops packets from the queue, formats `CSI_DATA` CSV lines using a fast integer-to-string encoder, and streams them out via `stdout` at 921,600 baud.
3. `traffic_gen_task()` pinned to **Core 0** periodically sends small UDP datagrams to the phone hotspot gateway. The phone's 802.11 MAC layer automatically returns 802.11 ACK frames, ensuring a sustained, high-rate (~100 Hz) downlink CSI stream without installing any app on the phone.

## 6. Preprocessing & Feature Extraction Pipeline

### 6.1 Noise Filtering Algorithms
1. **Hampel Filter:** A rolling window median filter with 3-sigma Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) replaces sudden transient spikes caused by AGC gain switches.
2. **Butterworth Bandpass Filter:** A 4th-order filter with passband $[0.3\text{ Hz}, 8.0\text{ Hz}]$ isolates human gait Doppler signatures while attenuating static channel DC offsets and high-frequency thermal noise.

### 6.2 Feature Vector Design
For each 1.0-second sliding window (100 packets @ 100 Hz), the engine extracts:
- **Subcarrier Statistical Moments:** Mean, Variance, Standard Deviation, and MAD for each of the 56 subcarriers.
- **Global Dynamics:** Total channel energy, global matrix variance.
- **Doppler Spectral Energy:** FFT-derived spectral power in the human locomotion band ($0.5\text{ Hz} - 2.5\text{ Hz}$) and fast movement band ($2.5\text{ Hz} - 6.0\text{ Hz}$).
- **Spectral Entropy:** Quantifying signal distribution complexity during human movement vs. static state.
- **Spatial Subcarrier Cross-Correlation:** Correlation coefficients between low-frequency and high-frequency subcarriers.

## 7. Machine Learning & Localization Algorithms

### 7.1 Presence & Activity Classification
- **Model:** Random Forest Classifier (100 trees, max depth = 10)
- **Input:** 232-dimensional engineered feature vector.
- **Classes:** `Empty Room`, `Standing`, `Sitting`, `Walking`.

### 7.2 Indoor Position Estimation
1. **Discrete Zone Fingerprinting (KNN):** Computes Euclidean distance between test CSI feature vectors and calibrated room grid fingerprints.
2. **Continuous $(X, Y)$ Regression:** Random Forest Regressor and Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) trained to output continuous spatial coordinates $(x, y)$.

### 7.3 Trajectory Smoothing via 2D Kalman Filter
To eliminate jitter and jump discontinuities, raw predicted positions $(z_x, z_y)$ pass through a 4-state Kalman Filter:

$$\mathbf{x}_t = \begin{bmatrix} x_t \\ y_t \\ v_{x,t} \\ v_{y,t} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{F} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & \Delta t & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & \Delta t \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{H} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
